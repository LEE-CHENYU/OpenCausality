## Interpretation Guide

- **Short-run** typically means within 1 year (or 1-4 quarters)
- **Long-run** typically means 3-5+ years
- Elasticities should be interpreted at the margin around current conditions
- Ranges reflect different identification strategies and sample periods
- Estimates outside these ranges are not necessarily wrong but require justification
